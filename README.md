# Daybook — Personal finance

A local-first income and expense tracker covering **October 2026 through October 2036**. The app can be installed on iPhone and Android and works offline after its first load.

## Install and use

The published app will be available at the GitHub Pages URL once Pages is enabled.

- **iPhone:** Open the app URL in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** Open the app URL in Chrome, tap the menu, then **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

On first launch, enter your name and choose your currency. The app saves entries in that device's browser storage. They do **not** sync between your iPhone and Android. Use **Export** on one device and **Import** on the other to copy a backup manually.

## Included

- Daily ledger for every day in the 121-month date range
- Income and expenses with amount and comment/note fields
- Monthly income, expenses, balance, active-day count, and category totals
- Requested categories and subcategories, with tools to add, rename, and remove your own
- JSON export and import for backups
- Installable web app with offline access after the first visit

## Privacy

The app has no server or account sync. It stores your ledger in browser local storage on each device. GitHub contains the app source only; entries are not sent to this repository. Clearing browser storage or switching devices does not carry your ledger over, so export backups regularly.
